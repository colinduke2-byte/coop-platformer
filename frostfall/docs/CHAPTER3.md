# Chapter 3: The Ember Crown (design for Phase 11; epic and mythic)

## Premise
The Hollow Kings bound the Winter with five chains. Goran told the truth: the chains were not iron. They were forged from
the **First Fire**, which the Kings chained *under Emberhold* so the world would never burn. When you broke the Winter's
hold (thaw, warden or crown), you also loosened the fire that held it. The mountain is waking. Fire and Winter were
the two halves of one balance, and the Anvil Court has known it for a thousand years.

## Structure (one quest, `crown`, with stages; the three faction quests feed it)
1. **Smoke on the Mountain**: after the Throne decision the Winter chapter ends and Hollowfrost sees smoke on the Peaks.
   Sigrid sends you up the **Peak Road**, a new gate in the north-east of the Hollow Reach that opens in Chapter 3.
2. **The Gate of Cinders**: arrive in Emberhold. Matriarch Ysolde reacts to your Winter choice (thaw: grateful but wary;
   warden: afraid and relieved; crown: she kneels, then plans). She tells you the Forge of the First Fire needs **three
   Ember Seals**, one from each house.
3. **Three Seals**: complete the three faction quests. Each leaves you a Seal (flags `sealDelvers`, `sealAnvil`, `sealWardens`):
   - The Silent Mines (Delvers): defeat Kragnar.
   - A Core for the Anvil (Anvil Court): deliver Kragnar's core, learn the true nature of the chains from Brannoch and Goran.
   - The Ashen Road (Wardens): clear three strongholds on the Ashen Peaks.
4. **The Forge of the First Fire**: with three seals, the sealed gate on the Ashen Peaks opens (dungeon, three rooms).
5. **The Ashen Sovereign**: the First Fire's will wears a crown of cinders. Three-phase boss. The factions you befriended
   send help into the fight: the one with the highest standing sends an ally effect (see below).
6. **The Last Choice**: Quench the Flame, Wear the Ember Crown, or Bind it to the Anvil. Final ending, credits.

## Faction help in the final fight (standing 45+ in the named faction)
- Anvil Court: your weapon is Emberforged for the fight (+15% damage).
- Delvers' Guild: lanterns light the arena; the Sovereign's cinder waves do 25% less damage.
- Ember Wardens: a Warden company holds the arena door; a banner restores 25 stamina each phase change.

## Endings (Chapter 3 is required for the full ending)
Final variants combine: which Winter ending you chose (thaw / warden / crown), the final choice (quench / crown / bind)
and the strongest faction. The chapter 2 cards say "CHAPTER TWO ENDS"; the credits come after the last choice.

| Final choice | Gist |
|---|---|
| Quench the Flame | Fire and Winter both end. Seasons return; Emberhold's furnaces cool. A world without gods, for better and worse. |
| Wear the Ember Crown | You hold the First Fire. With the Winter crown it becomes the Lord of Fire and Frost. |
| Bind it to the Anvil | The Court and the houses forge new chains with you as the first link. The balance holds. |

## New things
- NPCs: Sigrid gets new lines, Ysolde reacts per ending, Goran/Thessaly reveal the truth, Hesper marshals the road.
- Boss: **The Ashen Sovereign** (three phases, fire themed; uses cinder waves, falling embers, and summoned golems).
- Dungeon: **The Forge of the First Fire** (generated, three rooms: gauntlet, forge-trial, sovereign's hall).
- Items: **Ember Crown** (charm, the Wear ending), **Sovereign's Heart** (misc), **Seal of Cinders** (quest items).

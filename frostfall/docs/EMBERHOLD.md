# Emberhold: design for Phase 10 (names chosen by Claude, tone epic and mythic)

**The city.** Emberhold is carved into the flank of the Ashen Peaks, where the first fire of the world is said to sleep.
The Hollow Kings bound the Winter with five Hearts; the smiths of Emberhold forged the chains. When the Winter broke (or
was kept, or was sold), Emberhold's furnaces were the first to feel it.

## Districts (one city map, ~96x72 tiles, plus interiors)
| District | What is there |
|---|---|
| The Gate of Cinders | Arrival from the Ashen Peaks road. Wardens' post, a fast-travel fire, notice board. |
| Forge Row | Master smiths, the Great Anvil (reforge, socketing, tempering), rune-cutter, armoursmith. |
| The Delvers' Quarter | Delvers' Guild hall, mine gate to the Deep Mines, ore market, tavern "The Last Lantern". |
| The Court of Anvils | The Anvil Court's hall and throne, archive, a house for sale, the shrine of the First Flame. |
| Ashfall Market | General goods, alchemist, food, travelling traders, a small arena pit. |

## Factions (reputation 0-100 each; gaining one costs standing with another)
- **The Anvil Court**: rulers and master smiths. Lawful, proud, secretive about what the chains were made of.
- **The Delvers' Guild**: miners and tunnel-wardens. Practical, superstitious, loyal to each other first.
- **The Ember Wardens**: the city's soldiers and the road's guardians. Disciplined, tired, haunted by the last Winter.
Reputation unlocks gear, shop stock, quest branches and the Chapter 3 ending variants.

## Named people (~20)
- **Matriarch Ysolde Anvilborn** (Anvil Court, ruler): calm, ancient, hides the cost of the old chains.
- **Brannoch Redhammer** (master smith): gruff, teaches tempering; wants a Heart-forged ingot.
- **Tamsin Coalwright** (rune-cutter): sockets runes and gems; speaks in riddles.
- **Orrin Flintbeard** (Delvers' Guildmaster): worried about the Deep Mines going quiet.
- **Pell Quickpick** (Delver scout): comic relief, knows every shortcut, can become a companion.
- **Captain Hesper Vael** (Ember Wardens): wants the road to the Old Kingdom reopened.
- **Warden Corvin Ashe** (veteran): lost his company to the Winter; bitter, honest.
- **Maelis the Lantern-Keeper** (tavern): hears everything; sells rumours.
- **Old Goran Slagback** (retired smith): tells the true story of the chains.
- **Isolt Emberwick** (apprentice smith): player-ally, grows into a master across the chapter.
- **Dunmar the Gemcutter**, **Hild Soot** (alchemist), **Rook** (smuggler), **Sister Aurel** (shrine keeper),
  **Thessaly Vane** (archivist), **Garrow Pitt** (mine foreman), **Nessa Tallow** (cook),
  **Varro Stonefist** (arena master), **Ketil Ironsong** (bard), **Mother Brisa** (house agent).

## Deep Mines (first dungeon, 3 floors)
Collapsed ore-vaults, lantern puzzles, cave-ins, ember-moths, rock golems and ash-wights.
Boss: **Kragnar the Hollowed**, a miner-king bound to the lode, with a collapse-the-ceiling phase.
Reward: the Emberheart Ore, which Brannoch forges into the first **Emberforged** gear set.

## Services
Master smithing (new Emberforged tier, reforge, tempering), runes and gems (3 slots per item), guild contracts board,
house for sale (stash, forge, trophy wall), faction shop stocks, inn rest, arena pit.

## Opening beats (feeds Phase 11)
1. Arrive at the Gate of Cinders; Captain Vael checks your Heart-mark and asks which Winter you chose.
2. The Anvil Court hears the answer and reacts in character (Hearth: grateful but wary; Winter King: afraid;
   Cold Bargain: tries to buy you).
3. The Deep Mines have gone silent; the Delvers ask for help. That is the first dungeon and opens the chapter.

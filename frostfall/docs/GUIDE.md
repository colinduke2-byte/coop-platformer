# FROSTFALL: Player's Guide

*Break the winter.* This guide is for playing. Spoilers are marked; each section says what it spoils.

---

## 1. The first ten minutes

You start in **Hollowfrost Village**. Winter will not break, and the elder, **Sigrid**, has work for you.

1. Talk to people (**E**). Sigrid gives the main quest; others give small jobs. Open the journal (**O**) to see your quests, and the map (**M**) for markers.
2. Visit the shop and the lodge. Buy an **iron sword**, a shield and a few **health potions** (keys 1/2/3) before you leave.
3. Go out the village gate into **the Hollow Reach**, the big open world. Stay on the roads at first. Wolves and bandits hit hard before you have levels.
4. Fight near camps, not in the middle of them. Pull one or two enemies at a time.

**Rule of thumb:** if a fight takes more than half your potions, you are too early. Come back later.

## 2. Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / arrows | Move. Ice is slippery. |
| Space | Dodge roll. Brief invincibility, costs stamina. |
| J | Sword. Tap three times for a combo; the third hit is a heavy finisher. |
| U | Heavy attack: slow, staggers, breaks guards. |
| K (hold, release) | Bow. Hold to charge, release to fire. |
| L | Cast the selected spell. Q / Tab swaps spell. |
| 4 5 6 7 8 9 0 - | Quick-cast spells in order. |
| F (hold) | Block with a shield. Raise it just before a hit to **parry**. |
| C / Shift (hold) | Sneak. Sneak attacks hit 3× (melee) or 2× (bow). |
| T | Lock on to a target. |
| R / G | Shout / swap shout (after you earn more). |
| V | Switch arrows (plain, fire, barbed). |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Health / mana / stamina potion. |
| E / Enter | Talk, open, read, gather, rest, pick locks. |
| I / O / M / Esc | Pack and perks / journal / map / pause. |

Keys can be rebound in **Pause > System > Controls**. A gamepad and touch controls work too.

## 3. Fighting well

- **Stamina is your real health bar.** Swinging, rolling and blocking all spend it. When it hits zero you are helpless. Fight in short bursts.
- **Learn the telegraph.** Every enemy winds up before it hits. Roll sideways (not backward) just as the swing begins. A *perfect dodge* is rewarded.
- **Block and parry.** Hold F with a shield. Raising it right before a hit parries and opens the enemy up.
- **Use elements.** Many enemies are weak to something. Undead burn (fire); golems shatter under shock; frost-based foes shrug off frost. The bestiary (in the journal) lists weaknesses once you have met a creature.
- **Sneak first.** Sneak attacks triple your damage. Camps are far easier if you thin them quietly.
- **Crowds are dangerous.** Back into a doorway or a corridor so they come one at a time.
- **Day and night matter.** Night and storms make stealth easier and the world more dangerous. After dark **wolves become werewolves** and the restless dead of old places turn to **ghosts**; bandit crews sleep (they notice you at half range). The **moon** runs an eight-phase cycle (the clock and moon are in the top right): a **full moon** brings the pack out in force and every third one is a **blood moon**, when a great wolf hunts you. At a campfire you can **wait until dusk or dawn**. A **Hooded Lantern** (the smith) lights up the dark but makes you easier to notice.
- **Pick your difficulty** on the title screen (Options) or in Pause > System: Easy, Normal or Hard. Hard also turns gear wear on.
- **Read the name colours.** An enemy's name is coloured by how dangerous it is *to you right now* (your gear and level), and arriving somewhere far beyond you gives a warning. If it says DEADLY, come back stronger.

## 4. Getting stronger

Skills level **by use** (five of them), so use what you want to improve:

| Skill | Gets better from | Effect per level |
|---|---|---|
| One-Handed | swinging a sword | +7% sword damage, cheaper swings |
| Archery | shooting the bow | +7% arrow damage, faster draw |
| Destruction | casting attack spells | +7% spell damage, cheaper mana |
| Restoration | healing, wards, blocking | stronger heals and wards |
| Sneak | sneak attacks, pickpocketing | harder to detect, stronger backstabs |

- Levels come **slowly on purpose**: level 5 is around 125 sword hits and level 10 around 900. Grinding is real, and it is rewarded.
- Every two skill levels gained make a **character level**: you get a **perk point** and an attribute choice. Perks (I menu) are in chains of three per skill, unlocked at skill levels 2, 5 and 8 (for example Keen Edge, Duelist, Rending Blow).
- **Gear** matters as much as levels. Upgrade at the **forge** (reforge, temper, enchant). Armour has weight classes; heavy armour protects more.
- **Sockets**: better weapons and armour have slots for **gems** and **runes**. Runes add effects (burn, chill, shock, life-drain, thorns, warding). Set them at the rune-cutter in Emberhold.
- **Food and alchemy**: cook meals at campfires for buffs; brew potions. Always carry a few.
- **Companions**: hire *Ragna* in Hollowfrost, or recruit *Pell* in Emberhold. One at a time. Talk to them and choose *Orders*: stay close, hold the spot, or hunt on their own.
- **Capstone perks** (page 2 of the perk list) unlock at skill level 12: Unbroken, Deadeye, Archmage, Second Wind and Assassin.
- **Inns and chartmakers** (Reedwick, Skarn Hold): a room heals, saves and sleeps until morning; a rumour names a place you have not found; a chart reveals the map around you.
- **Dungeon rooms to watch for**: spike halls (the floor glows red before it strikes), flooded halls (slow, with leeches) and ambush rooms (a chest in a suspiciously quiet room).

## 5. The world

### The Hollow Reach (start)
A large open land of forest, ruins, camps, dens, standing stones, hamlets, watchtowers and **barrows** (generated dungeons). Everything you clear stays cleared; the map fills in as you explore. Use **campfires** to rest and fast travel.

- **Bounty board** in the village: repeatable jobs for gold.
- **Hamlets** have trappers, fishers and prospectors with their own jobs.
- **Champions** are tough elite enemies that guard good loot.
- **Fishing, hunting and digging** (treasure maps lead you to dig spots) all give rewards.
- **The Hollow Arena**: wave challenges for gold and trophies.

### The seven other regions
Three open as the story progresses (see section 6). **The Weeping Fens, the Stormcrown Highlands and the Glasswood can be walked to from the start**: the roads leave the Hollow Reach at its south-west, north-east and south. The Underdeep is entered by the Deep Stair in the Ashen Peaks. They are dangerous. Go when you are ready.

| Region | What it is like |
|---|---|
| **The Ashen Peaks** | Lava, foundries, cinder enemies. Home of the forge-city **Emberhold**. |
| **The Frozen Coast** | Wrecks, lighthouses, drowned things. Ends at **Tidebreak Hall**. |
| **The Old Kingdom** | Courtyards, sentinels, ghosts of a lost court. Ends in **the Sepulchre**. |
| **The Weeping Fens** | A drowned bog of black water and boardwalks. The stilt village **Reedwick** has an inn, a smith and a chartmaker; the **Sunken Barrow** ends in the **Mire Mother**. Poison everywhere; fire works well. |
| **The Glasswood** | A crystalline forest of ringing glass trees. **Lantern Glade** has an inn, a smith, a mapmaker and a Moon-Reader who only talks after dark; the **Hart Spire** ends in the **Hartking** (a barred gate: find the key in the first hall). Lightning and fire work well. |
| **The Underdeep** | The caverns under the Ashen Peaks: glowing fungus, cave weavers, hollowed Delvers. **Lanternfall** has Orm's ferry up to Emberhold; the **Lode Chasm** ends in the **Lode Colossus**. Dark: carry a lantern. |
| **Saltmarket** | The Frozen Coast's harbour city, two guilds (Tide and Smugglers: they are rivals), a tailor and sea quests. **Seaweed Cove** below ends in **Captain Brinegut**. |
| **The Stormcrown Highlands** | Windswept heath under a storm that never leaves. **Skarn Hold** is a clan ring with an inn, a smith and a chartmaker; the **Stormspire** ends in the **Storm Giant**. Lightning everywhere; frost works well. |

Gear sets (two or three matching pieces) give a bonus: Emberforged, the Court, Delvers, Wardens, Prism, Deepforged, Tide-Guild. Storms chill you unless you wear warm armour or stand by a fire. Silver weapons and daylight both hurt the undead. Moonpetals only bloom at night.

Each region has its own camps, caves, hamlets, loot and a boss. Check the **map tab** and press **R** to cycle regions.

## 6. The story (spoilers: structure only, not the ending)

**Chapter 1: The Hollow King.** Find the crypt in the forest, defeat **Jarl Valdrek** and take the Frostheart. Side jobs here include **Mirra's Remedy**, **Asta's Locket**, **The Pale Alpha** (Grimfang, in Frostwind Pass), **Wolves at the Gate** and **The Bridge Troll's Toll**.

**Chapter 2: The Four Hearts.** Three more Hearts and a throne. The four Heart dungeons are:

| Heart | Place | Gift |
|---|---|---|
| Rime Heart | The Glacial Maw | +20 stamina, perfect dodge freezes foes |
| Iron Heart | Ironwatch Keep | +8% armour, +10% melee |
| Tide Heart | The Drowned Chapel | cheaper rolls, faster stamina |
| Root Heart | The Rootvault | slow regeneration, stronger potions |

When you hold all four, open the **Winter Throne**, face the Long Winter, and decide its fate (three endings: thaw, warden or crown).

**Chapter 3: The Ember Crown** (required for the full ending). Smoke rises on the Ashen Peaks. Take the Peak Road to **Emberhold**, speak to **Matriarch Ysolde**, and earn three **Ember Seals**, one from each faction, by finishing their quests (the Delvers' Guild: *The Silent Mines*; the Anvil Court: *A Core for the Anvil*; the Ember Wardens: *The Ashen Road*). The seals open the **Forge of the First Fire**, where the **Ashen Sovereign** waits, then comes the last choice.

### The factions of Emberhold
Reputation runs 0 to 100 with each. Helping one house can cost goodwill with another.

| House | Style | Help in the final fight (at 45+) |
|---|---|---|
| **The Anvil Court** | rulers and master smiths | your weapon fights harder |
| **The Delvers' Guild** | miners and tunnel-wardens | the hall is lit and the Sovereign's cinder waves hit weaker |
| **The Ember Wardens** | the city's soldiers | a banner restores stamina each phase |

High standing also improves shop stock, forging (the Great Anvil sometimes makes a *masterwork*), quest branches and your ending.

## 7. Bosses

| Boss | Where | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| **Jarl Valdrek** | Crypt of the Hollow King | Fight him in the open; roll his wind-up swings. Bring fire. |
| **Grimfang** | Frostwind Pass | Fast. Fire hurts him. You can spare him (see his quest). |
| **The four Heart guardians** | the four Heart dungeons | Read their telegraphs; each has a pattern you can learn in one fight. |
| **Kragnar the Hollowed** | the Deep Mines (Emberhold) | Frost is his weakness; fire is resisted. Watch for his leaps and spikes. |
| **The Ashen Sovereign** | Forge of the First Fire | Three phases (the Crowned, the Cinder Storm, the Pyre). Frost damage is best; fire does almost nothing. Bring faction help and plenty of potions. |
| **Admiral Veyl** | Tidebreak Hall | Shock and fire work; frost does little. Fights get easier after his halfway phase. |
| **The Hollow King** | the Sepulchre | Three phases (the Throne, the Court, the Memory). He hits hard and fast. Level up and bring your best gear first. |

Bosses summon helpers. Kill the helpers if they crowd you; otherwise focus the boss.

**General boss rules:** heal early, never greed past the third swing of a combo, and keep stamina for rolling. If a boss kills you, note which attack did it and plan a roll for that.

## 8. Endings

There are many endings (36 variants in Chapter 3 alone). They depend on:
1. What you did with the Frostheart and Grimfang.
2. How you ended the Winter (thaw, warden or crown).
3. Your last choice: **Quench the Flame**, **Wear the Ember Crown**, or **Bind it to the Anvil** (needs a house to trust you).
4. Which faction you stand best with.

After the credits you can **start New Game+** with your progress and stronger enemies. There is also a **Daily Challenge** on the title screen: a fixed world and two modifiers per day, with a score board.

## 9. Tips and tricks

- **Save often**: three slots, with automatic backups. You can save mid-boss.
- **Read everything**: books and signs reveal lore, and some hint at hidden places.
- **Not every chest is a chest**: mimics exist. Listen and watch.
- **Carry a lockpick and some gold**: locked doors and chests hold good items.
- **Sell junk**, but keep ore, gems and runes. You will want them for sockets.
- **Whaler's skates** (from a coast fisher, 250 gold) make travel on ice much faster.
- If you are stuck, the **journal** shows your current objective and the map shows a marker.
- If it is too hard, lower the difficulty in the pause menu. If it is too easy, add a **modifier** in a new game (for example Glass Cannon).

## 10. Quick reference: damage types

| Type | Good against | Bad against |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | undead, wolves, frost creatures | fire creatures (cinder foes, the Sovereign) |
| Frost | fire and cinder creatures, the Sovereign | frost creatures, the Hollow King, Veyl |
| Shock | golems, knights, armoured foes, Veyl | few |

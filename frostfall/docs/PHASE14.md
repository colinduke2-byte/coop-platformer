# Phase 14 - Breadth (what gets built, in order of value)

1. **New enemies (12)**, one or two per region, each with its own counter-play and sprite:
   Ash Hound (fast, burns), Cinder Smith (hammer slam + shield of sparks), Magma Slime (splits), Lava Wraith (phases through lava),
   Ice Harpooner (pulls you in), Wreck Crab (armoured, flips), Tide Hag (summons drowned), Frost Barnacle (stationary turret),
   Court Phantom (teleports), Hollow Herald (buffs allies), Bone Sentinel (guards a door), Moss Stalker (ambush).
2. **New weapon class: crossbow** (slow, heavy bolt, pierce) and **warhammer** (armour-breaker, 2H).
3. **Runes**: a second socket kind (gems add stats; runes add effects: ignite, chill, chain-shock, lifedrain). Tamsin sets them.
4. **New spells and shouts**: Ember Nova, Glacier Wall, Chain Lightning; shout "Hearthcall" (heals allies) and "Cinderstep" (dash through enemies).
5. **Crafting**: mining nodes (pickaxe swing minigame, timed taps) and smithing minigame at the Emberforge (quality roll).
6. **Faster travel**: a sled you can summon on the ice shelf and the Peak Road (speed boost, can't fight).
7. **Modes**: Boss Rush (all bosses in a row, between-fight shop), Faction Challenges (daily tasks per house), more Daily modifiers.
8. **Music**: three new tracks (Ashen Peaks, Frozen Coast, Old Kingdom) and ambience per region.
